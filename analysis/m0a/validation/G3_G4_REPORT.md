## G3a. The 20 m DEMs vs HiRISE

HiRISE is smoothed (σ 10 m) before each comparison. A positive value means HiRISE is higher.

| DEM | Median diff, box | MAD, box | Corridor median / MAD | Slope correlation | Best horizontal shift (search ±80 m) | Robust σ at 0 shift → at best shift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| JPL CTX DEM 20 m | −0.15 m | 1.75 m | +0.23 / 0.50 m | 0.82 | **52 m E, 12 m S** | 2.60 → 1.85 m |
| USGS TRN CTX DTM 20 m | −1.00 m | 2.04 m | −0.23 / 1.18 m | 0.79 | **48 m E, 20 m S** | 3.03 → 1.83 m |

- **Vertical:** both CTX DEMs agree with HiRISE within the gate limit (MAD ≤ 5 m).
- **Horizontal (a lead, not a correction):** two independently produced CTX DEMs both fit HiRISE better when sampled about 50 m east and 12–20 m south.
  - Meaning: features in the CTX DEMs sit about 2.5 CTX cells away from where HiRISE has them, which is consistent with the CTX/PLACES residual pattern below.
  - Why it is still weak: the improvement is moderate (σ 2.6 → 1.85 m), and the measure is a robust spread, not a sharp correlation peak.
  - The earlier ROI-A investigation (`science/s4_registration_investigation.md`) also reached the edge of its search at about E +50 m, N +15 m, but called it inconclusive. That was a different method and pair (CTX ortho vs CTX DEM).
  - **Nothing was corrected.** This is relevant to the open G1/S-4 item for ROI-A, and the ROI-A bundles were not changed.

**Update, 2026-11-15:** the controlled USGS CTX 6 m orthomosaic is from the same soc_008 product family as the
TRN CTX DTM. Image-to-image matching against the HiRISE ortho (NCC 0.89–0.90, sharp peaks; see
`analysis/regional/d3d62fc34c56/regional.json`) gives only 1.2 m E / −1.2 m N at M0–A and 1.1 m / 0.3 m at B. The
~50 m DEM-to-DEM "best shift" above is therefore most likely an artefact of the elevation-spread metric (a flat
minimum and a resolution mismatch), not a real horizontal offset. Treat it as **unconfirmed**. Do not cite it as a
CTX registration error.

## G3b. HiRISE stereo-pair seams

The USGS HiRISE DTM is a blended mosaic ("blend40") of stereo pairs. Pairs C, W and DL all cover the whole corridor.

| Comparison | Corridor median | MAD |
|---|---|---|
| pair C − pair W | −1.70 m | 0.27 m |
| pair C − pair DL | +0.67 m | 0.27 m |
| pair W − pair DL | **+2.41 m** | 0.24 m |
| **mosaic − pair C** | **0.000 m in 100 % of corridor cells (and of the whole box)** | 0 |

- **Gate decision:** the gate document defines the condition as "no seam step > 2 m along the corridor". Over the M0–A box the mosaic **is** pair C alone, so no seam lies in the corridor. **G3 passes on that direct test.**
- **The proxy I first coded fails.** It required every inter-pair offset to be ≤ 2 m, and W − DL is 2.41 m. That proxy is stricter than the definition, so I replaced it with the direct test. Both results are kept in the JSON (`corridor_single_pair: true`, `pair_offset_proxy_ok: false`).
- **Carry forward:** the pairs disagree by constant offsets of up to 2.4 m. This is a vertical uncertainty of the HiRISE product, and it must appear in any route report.

| HiRISE DTM 1 m | −0.002 m | 0.07 m | **0.27 m** | all within ±0.25 m |

| TRN CTX DTM | −1.39 m | 2.14 m | 6.26 m | drops ≈ −1.4 m, sampling positions +4.1 to +4.5 m |

- **Caveat (important):** the HiRISE agreement is *too* good to count as independent. PLACES localises the rover on HiRISE orthos and DTMs, so these elevations very likely come from the same terrain model. This shows the catalog positions and the DTM are consistent. It does **not** independently validate the vertical datum, which stays UNVERIFIED.
- The CTX pattern fits a horizontal offset. The depot is flat, so residuals there are stable. The sampling positions are on a slope, where a ~50 m shift shows up as elevation error.

## What this means for G5 and routing

- **HiRISE (2 m grid) looks suitable as the analysis DEM:**
  - no NoData;
  - no seam in the corridor;
  - consistent with its ortho to < 2 m;
  - consistent with the catalog rover positions to 0.27 m.
- **CTX 20 m is not:** it is coarse (about 20 cells along the route), and it is probably offset by about 50 m relative to the HiRISE/PLACES frame.
- **Still open:**
  - vertical datum (not independently checked);
  - the 2.4 m vertical disagreement between pairs (reported, not corrected);
  - G4 human sign-off;
  - G5 decision;
  - G6 connectivity, since 3.7 % of the HiRISE box is steeper than 24.2°.
